About

I'm Hafid, an undergraduate Information Systems student based in East Java, Indonesia. I got into tech because I wanted to understand how everything connects — from the database to the browser, from a server config to a game loop.

I approach every domain as a student first. I don't believe in being locked into one discipline, especially early on. The generalist mindset helps me connect dots that specialists sometimes miss.

Currently building AksaraLoka and ChikaAI — two projects that are teaching me more than any course has.
